Quality Control

Our Installation Standards

Every project goes through rigorous preparation and post-heat finishing to ensure zero edge lifting or peeling over time.

1

Deep Decontamination

Multi-stage clay bar wash and isopropyl alcohol wipe down to eliminate microscopic tar and wax.

2

Precision Dismantling

Careful removal of door handles, bumpers, and trim pieces for seamless edge tucking.

3

Master Application

Installed in a climate-controlled sterile bay by certified wrap specialists using knifeless tape.

4

Post-Heat Curing

Thermal post-heating of all stretched areas to permanently lock film memory into recesses.
