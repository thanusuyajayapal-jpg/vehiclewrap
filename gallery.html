Behind The Scenes

The VelocityCraft Customization Journey

Every portfolio masterpiece follows our rigorous 4-step master installation protocol to ensure perfection.

01

Consultation & Design

We discuss your aesthetic vision, select film swatches, digital renderings, and map out the custom finish.

02

Decontamination

Multi-stage wash, clay bar treatment, and isopropyl alcohol wipe down to ensure absolute zero dust adhesion.

03

Precision Application

Certified wrap masters meticulously apply, heat-stretch, and tuck film edges inside all door jambs and panels.

04

Quality Audit & Cure

Post-heating edge inspection, ceramic top-coat sealing, and rigorous quality audit before final vehicle handover.
